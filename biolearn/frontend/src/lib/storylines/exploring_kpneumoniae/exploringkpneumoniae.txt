================================================================================
EXPLORING K. PNEUMONIAE - WGS ANALYSIS TUTORIAL
================================================================================

Storyline: Exploring K. pneumoniae
Dataset: SRR36708862 (Klebsiella pneumoniae)
Platform: Illumina NextSeq 2000 (2x150bp paired-end)

================================================================================
COMMANDS
================================================================================

Phase 1: Quality Control
------------------------
Step 1: Check Sequencing Statistics
seqkit stats SRR36708862_1.fastq.gz SRR36708862_2.fastq.gz > o_seqkit_stats.txt

Step 2: View Statistics Results
cat o_seqkit_stats.txt

Step 3: Quality Control Report
fastqc SRR36708862_1.fastq.gz SRR36708862_2.fastq.gz -o o_fastqc/

Phase 2: Read Preprocessing
---------------------------
Step 4: Adapter Trimming
trimmomatic PE -threads 2 -phred33 SRR36708862_1.fastq.gz SRR36708862_2.fastq.gz o_trimmomatic/SRR36708862_R1_paired.fq.gz o_trimmomatic/SRR36708862_R1_unpaired.fq.gz o_trimmomatic/SRR36708862_R2_paired.fq.gz o_trimmomatic/SRR36708862_R2_unpaired.fq.gz ILLUMINACLIP:TruSeq3-PE.fa:2:30:10 SLIDINGWINDOW:4:15 MINLEN:36

Phase 3: Genome Assembly
------------------------
Step 5: De Novo Assembly
unicycler -1 o_trimmomatic/SRR36708862_R1_paired.fq.gz -2 o_trimmomatic/SRR36708862_R2_paired.fq.gz -o o_unicycler/

Step 6: Visualize Assembly Graph
bandage image o_unicycler/assembly.gfa o_bandage.png

Phase 4: Assembly Quality Assessment
------------------------------------
Step 7: Assembly Metrics
quast o_unicycler/assembly.fasta -o o_quast/

Step 8: Genome Completeness
checkm2 predict --input o_unicycler/ --output-directory o_checkm2/ -x fasta

Step 9: Plasmid Identification
plasmidfinder.py -i o_unicycler/assembly.fasta -x -o o_plasmidfinder

Phase 5: AMR Screening & Typing
-------------------------------
Step 10: AMR Gene Detection
abricate --db ncbi o_unicycler/assembly.fasta -o o_abricate/

Step 11: MLST Typing
mlst o_unicycler/assembly.fasta > o_mlst/mlst_result.tab

Phase 6: Genome Annotation
--------------------------
Step 12: Gene Annotation
prokka --outdir o_prokka --prefix PROKKA o_unicycler/assembly.fasta
